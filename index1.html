1. 1
    Recite默念 Please put your palms together and sincerely recite “Namo Guan Shi Yin Bodhisattva of Great Compassion” three times in silence. 请合掌，虔诚地默念「南无观世音菩萨」三遍。
  2. 2
    State your question说明事项 Confess sincerely to Guanyin Bodhisattva, stating clearly the question you wish to ask: “I, Disciple ... wish to ask for the compassionate guidance of Avalokiteshvara (state the matter)” 诚心地向观音菩萨启白，明确地提出您的问题：
    弟子。。。有一件事想请求观世音菩萨的慈悲引导「说明事项」
    • Ask about one matter at a time.一次只问一件事。
    • Name the person, situation and timeframe, e.g. “Will my job application succeed this year?” rather than “How is my career?”说明人、事和时间，例如「我今年的工作申请能否成功？」而不是「我的事业如何？」
    • Ask what you should do, not only yes or no.可请求指引应如何做，而不只是问是否。
  3. 3
    Draw your lot求签 Shake or click on the divination flask. 摇动或单击签桶。
