第 29 签吉凶：中签

签诗
宝剑出匣耀光明在匣全然不惹尘今得贵人携出现有威有势众人钦
解曰
宝剑出匣光辉万里贵人指引无不观美
诗意

此卦宝剑出匣之象。

凡事有威有势也。

签诗

像宝剑一样，以前在匣中就一尘不染，现在出匣更是明洁光亮。得到贵人提携，也就更有威势，也令众人十分钦佩。

签解

此签为中签。

像出匣的宝剑一样，光辉照万里。如果遇上贵人指引，必将获得成功，让众人赞美不已。

The supreme sword, unsheathed, dazzles,

Interpret­ation

The supreme sword is drawn, dazzling many for miles.

Meaning Of This Lot

A powerful sword is drawn from its sheath.

A show of power and might.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Like a precious sword that is untainted by even a speck of dust when in the sheath. Now, it is even brighter and shinier when out of the sheath. With the help from a nobleman, it is even more powerful, and admired by everyone.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Like an unsheathed precious sword that glitters and shines for miles.

If you meet a nobleman and are guided by him, you'll certainly attain success and be praised by all men.
