第 40 签吉凶：中签

签诗
红轮西坠兔东升阴长阳消百事亨若是女人宜望用增添财禄福其心
解曰
阴利女人不宜男子若是求谋先凶后吉
诗意

此卦阴长阳消之象。

凡事先难后易也。

签诗

太阳西下，月亮东升，阴长阳消，百事消停。如果能让女人发挥更多作用，则必定能增添财禄，自己也会感到更幸福。

签解

此签为中签。

阴柔的环境更有利于女人，不适宜于男人。

如果是问谋划中的事情，则必定是先凶后吉。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 40Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

The red wheel sets in the West as the rabbit rises in the East.

Things remain even as the lunar grows in strength and the solar wanes.

If a woman is seeking to divine her future,

She will be blessed with wealth, riches and fortune.

Interpret­ation

A “Yin” Lot is favorable if the question involves a female.

Otherwise, for a male seeking his fortune, it will be ominous before it will turn better.

Meaning Of This Lot

A strong “Yin” (Moon) presiding over a declining “Yang” (Sun). There will be difficulties initially but these will soon pass and things will get easier.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

When the sun sets in the west, the moon will rise in the east. With the Yin growing stronger, the Yang will weaken, and everything will come to a stop. Therefore, if females are allowed to take on greater roles, it will surely bring on more wealth and good fortune, and one will feel happier too.

Divination of The Lot

This is an inauspicious lot.

A “Yin” environment is more advantageous to females and is unsuitable for males. If you are asking about what is being planned, it will initially be bad, before turning into auspicious.
