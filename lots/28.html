第 28 签吉凶：中签

签诗
东边月上正婵娟顷刻云遮亦暗存或有圆时还有缺更言非者亦闲言
解曰
浮云遮月不须疑惑等待云收便见收获
诗意

此卦月被云遮之象。

凡事昏迷未定也。

签诗

东边天空月色正好，顷刻间却被浮云遮蔽变暗。正如圆缺明暗是互相转化的，眼前的这些非议也不过是闲言罢了。

签解

此签为中签。

即使月亮被浮云遮蔽了，也不要产生疑惑。等到浮云散去，自然会见到好结果。

Lot Verse

The pale moon rises from the east,

Obscured at times by the clouds, unseen yet present.

At times, it’s round. At times, it’s crescent.

The truth, hidden by idle talk, is not present.

Interpret­ation

The fleeting clouds cover the moon. Do not hesitate.

When the clouds have passed, you will see the rewards.

Meaning Of This Lot

The moon is obscured by clouds. Things are hazy and uncertain at the moment.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

The moonlight in the eastern sky was so beautiful, but suddenly it was obscured by the floating clouds and became dark. Just like the natural transformation of the moon, which interchanges between being bright when it is full and dark when it is not, the criticisms in front of us are merely idle words.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Even though the moon is covered by the floating clouds, do not have any doubts. When the clouds disperse, you will naturally be able to see good results.
