第 25 签吉凶：中签

签诗
过了忧危事几重从今再立永无空宽心自有宽心计得遇高人立大功
解曰
讼终有理病得安全出入求谋古井逢泉
诗意

此卦古井逢泉之象。

凡事贵人成就也。

签诗

经历了不少忧患险情，从今再度创业不会落空了。少了担忧自然该有不担忧的计划，值得宽慰的是，如果遇到高人相助，还可能获得大的成功。

签解

此签为中签。

如果打官司能占理；如果得病则能安然康复；如果做事则应多多地谋划、考虑周全，如果这样便能重获帮助和支持。

事项
  • 家宅情况一般
  • 自身情况不顺畅
  • 投资理财有阻滞
  • 经商买卖有阻滞
  • 工农商业不利，应全盘考虑计划
  • 工作事业放宽心胸，多虚心请教
  • 学业考试多用功并多请教
  • 爱情婚姻和谐融合
  • 怀孕生育小心谨慎
  • 出门在外有阻隔
  • 畜养动物不利
  • 寻人阻隔
  • 寻失物难以找到
  • 官司诉讼有理
  • 迁徙搬家不适合
  • 转换变更不宜改变
  • 治病健康应积善祈福庇佑
  • 祖坟牌位不安稳
签语

此卦古井逢泉之象。所祈求之事获得贵人的协助而得到成就。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 25Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

One has met with sorrows, dangers and hardships,

Henceforth, plans revived will prosper.

A broad mind with a broader scheme,

Success will come with help from a distinguished patron.

Interpret­ation

Lawsuit will be settled. The sick will be cured and saved. Like the well that is replenished is how you will find your fortune.

Meaning Of This Lot

Just like an old well that is replenished with spring water, with help from distinguished patrons, you will be successful.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Having gone through many worries and crises, starting a business again, you will survive and not fail. With less worry, it is natural to have a plan with no apprehension and it is reassuring that you may even achieve great success if you encounter help from a benefactor.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

If you have lawsuits, you can justify; if you get sick, you will recover safely; if you do things, you should plan carefully and consider all possibilities, and if so, you can get help and support again.
