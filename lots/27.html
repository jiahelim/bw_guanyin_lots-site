一谋一用一番机虑后思前不敢为时到贵人相助力如山壁立可安居

改旧成新寒花遇春从前阻滞今得称心
诗意

此卦屋好墙壁之象。

签诗

所有的计划都流于文字，仍然瞻前顾后不敢施行。时机来时，得到贵人的帮助，方能立身坚固，平平安安。

此签为中签。

把旧日的东西换成崭新的，就会像寒冬里枯萎的花草遇到春风一样，会立即生机勃勃。虽然从前多有阻碍，不太顺利，但如今终于能够称心遂意了。

Lot Verse

Every move is carefully planned,

Until such time when distinguished help arrive,

Interpret­ation

Replace old with new. Like the arrival of Spring when flowers bloom again, what was holding you back is now gone and things will go as you wish..

Meaning Of This Lot

A good house with strong walls, everything will be stable with minimum risk or danger.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

All the plans are mere words, and still you are overly cautious, afraid to execute it. When opportunity comes, and with the help of a nobleman, you will be able to stand firm and enjoy peace.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Replace old things with brand new ones. It is like withered flowers and plants in winter that meet the spring wind and they immediately become full of vitality. Even though there were lots of obstacles in the past and things were not going too smoothly, but now things can finally be satisfactory to one's liking.
