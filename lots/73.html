Lot Verse

Awaken by a sudden clap of thunder in Spring,

Worms and insects call and leap from the soil.

As the comings and goings gather together,

In one sweep, they are transformed into a dragon.

Interpret­ation

As the thunder roars, the wheel of fortune turns.

In one quick sweep, things change and one is soon in front of the celestial gate.

Meaning Of This Lot

The thunder woke all hibernating creatures.

Your affairs will be auspicious because of the help you will receive from others.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

The spring thunder rumbles, and the hibernating worms sing and roll over from the soil, then they can feel the liberty to come and go. If one day another qualitative change occurs, worms can even become dragons.

Divination of The Lot

This is an auspicious lot.

When the sudden thunder of spring awakens, fortune starts to change; from then on, you can get around with ease. When the day of change happens, one will definitely attain huge success.
