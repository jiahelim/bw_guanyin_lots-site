第 72 签吉凶：中签

签诗
养蜂须用求他蜜只怕遭触尾上针须是眼前有异路暗里深如荆棘林
解曰
事须仔细不用强求结蜂采蜜有甚来由
诗意

此卦结蜂采蜜、求甜得苦之象。

诸事劳心费力，凡事防范灾殃也。

签诗

养峰当然想得到峰蜜，只是恐怕会被蜂尾针螫。虽然目前有别的路可走，但暗里仍然荆棘密布。

签解

此签为中签。

做事必须仔细，不要强求，如同与蜜蜂结伴去采蜜一样，哪会有什么好结果啊?

  • 工作事业劳心费力

  • 爱情婚姻会被阻隔
  • 怀孕生育虚惊
  • 出门在外到达目的地的时间延迟
  • 畜养动物收成较少
  • 寻人难找到
  • 寻失物过程中有凶险
  • 官司情况亏输
  • 迁徙搬家不要搬迁
  • 转换变更不宜转变
  • 疾病情况宜作祈福回向
  • 祖坟牌位宜修缮
签语

此卦结蜂采蜜、求甜得苦之象。所祈求之事不仅劳心费力，还需防范祸殃。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 72Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Raise bees for the sake of their honey,

The only fear are the stings from the tails.

Although there are other ways to make a living,

There are hidden thistles and thorns that one does not see.

Interpret­ation

Plans need to be thought through.

Do not ask for the sky.

Raise the bees for honey and there will be rewards for your labor.

Meaning Of This Lot

Gathering bees in order to collect honey, a bitter effort to collect some sweetness.

A lot of hard work and effort is required.

Take precautions to avoid disasters.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

We raise bees for their honey, only that we fear being stung by them. Although we have alternate paths currently, we know there are hidden thorns we do not see.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

When working we need to be meticulous and not ask for the sky. It is akin to raising bees for honey, what good will come out of it?
