Your share of fortune is much the same as others.

Meaning Of This Lot

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Unique and extraordinary, the majestic pagoda stands tall, shining constantly in all 8 directions with brilliance. It is advisable for you to devote yourself to earnest worship, do more virtuous deeds, then Heaven will naturally bless you with good fortune and happiness.

Divination of The Lot

This is an auspicious lot.

All these worldly good fortunes, everyone sees it frequently. Do not expend a great deal of effort chasing after it. Everything is predestined in previous life.
