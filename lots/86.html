第 86 签吉凶：上签

签诗
春来花发映阳台万里车来进宝财若得禹门三级浪恰如平地一声雷
解曰
朝帝受职如贫得宝谋望从心卦中第一
诗意

此卦上朝见帝之象。

凡事大吉大利也。

签诗

春来花开辉映门庭，招贤车来时财宝随至。如果龙门涌起波浪，就会身化为龙，像一声春雷名扬天下。

签解

此签为上签。

若从政走仕途，能够得到职位；若是贫穷则可获得财宝，谋划展望未来的事业，也可以称心如意。这是所有卦中第一位的好卦。

事项

此签所昭示的神机是从心所欲，万事都吉祥顺利。

签语

此卦上朝见帝之象。所祈求之事非常吉祥。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 86Prophesy: Auspicious

Lot Verse

On the balcony, the flowers bloom as Spring arrives.

Treasures brought by vessels from miles away.

Once over the dragon’s gate, the waves surged thrice,

Shakes the earth like a sudden clap of thunder.

Interpret­ation

The emperor has appointed you.

This is like receiving endless wealth.

Everything will work out for the best.

This Lot is the best of all.

Meaning Of This Lot

An appointment conferred by the emperor (someone in higher authority).

Everything will be very auspicious and favorable.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Spring flowers bloom and brighten your door, and a chariot arrives, bringing loads of treasure. If a big wave surges at the dragon's gate, you will swiftly rise like a dragon, like a clap of thunder that brings you fame to the world.

Divination of The Lot

This is an auspicious lot.

If you go into politics or government service, you can get a position; if you are poor you will receive treasure, and all plans for your future All things will work out to be the best, this is the best lot of all.

Celestial Message

Reveals the divine sign that you can do whatever you wish, and everything will be auspicious and smooth.
