第 60 签吉凶：下签

签诗
抱薪救火大皆燃烧遍三千亦复然若问荣华并出入不如收拾枉劳心
解曰
有千般计不如莫动一旦相惹恐祸归身
诗意

此卦抱薪救火之象。

凡事亦自谨防也。

签诗

抱柴草去救火，会燃得更旺，甚至会无止尽地燃烧。如果要问荣华富贵和出入是否平安，不如收起自己的枉然心机。

签解

此签为下签。

即使有很多好的选择，也不如保持现状，否则一旦变化，恐怕要惹祸上身。

  • 工作事业守业为宜

  • 怀孕生育注意安全
  • 出门在外在途中耽搁
  • 畜养动物有损失
  • 寻人毫无音信
  • 寻失物在西方
  • 官司诉讼会亏输
  • 迁徙搬家吉利
  • 转换变更静待时机
  • 治病健康预防惊吓
  • 祖坟牌位维持现状
签语

此卦抱薪救火之象。所祈求之事还需自己小心谨慎防护。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 60Prophesy: Inauspicious

Lot Verse

Add fuel to a fire and now it burns more fiercely,

The bright flames spread for miles, sparing nothing.

On the pursuits of riches and fame,

Why not save the trouble and forget?

Interpret­ation

Why bother with so many schemes, why not let things remain?

Doing too much may someday inevitably create problems that will be hard to get rid of.

Meaning Of This Lot

Trying to extinguish a fire with firewood.

You must take precautions to protect yourself.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

When you carry firewood to put out a fire, it will burn more fiercely, and even burn endlessly. If you are asking about wealth, fame and safe passage wherever you go, you might as well put away your scheming efforts.

Divination of The Lot

This is an inauspicious lot.

Even if you have many good choices, it is better to maintain the status quo.

Otherwise, you may get into trouble once changes occur.
