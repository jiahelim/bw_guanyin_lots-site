第 18 签吉凶：上签

签诗
金乌西坠兔东升日夜循环至古今僧道得知无不利士农工商各从心
解曰
心中无事所作有功如弓上箭一发当空
诗意

此卦阴阳消长之象。

凡事遂意之兆也。

签诗

太阳西下，月亮东升，日夜循环、阴阳消长是从古到今的事。僧人道人知道了这一规律就能通天彻地，士农工商懂得这规律就能各随所愿。

签解

此签为上签。

心中坦荡，不用多疑，认真做自己该做的事情。事情做好了功业自然会成，就像弦弓上的箭一样，一当射出自然会在空中前行。

Lot: 18Prophesy: Auspicious

Lot Verse

The Golden Crow sets as the Jade Rabbit rises,

Days and nights cycle since the world begins.

The Buddhist or Tao practitioners will gain much,

So will the scholar, peasant, worker or merchant.

Interpret­ation

Free your heart of cares. Your efforts will be rewarded.

Like an arrow on a bow, it will be a clear shot on release.

Meaning Of This Lot

The exchange of Yin and Yang symbolizes the natural order.

Harmony will prevail as things take its natural cause.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

The sun sets in the west and the moon rises from the east. The cycle of day & night, the ebb and flow of yin & yang are known facts since ancient times. When Monks & Taoists know this law, they can understand every phenomenon on earth. So scholars, peasants, worker and merchants who know this, will get what they wish for.

Divination of The Lot

This is an auspicious lot.

Be magnanimous in your heart, there is no need to be overly suspicious, earnestly do the tasks you should do. Once these tasks have been completed, your work will naturally succeed. This is just like an arrow on a stretched bow string, once released, the arrow will naturally soar into the sky.
