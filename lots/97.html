第 97 签吉凶：中签

签诗
当风点烛空疏影恍惚铺成镜里花几度儿童来收拾岂知收拾总成空
解曰
富贵在天贫穷是命不用求谋皆是前定
诗意

此卦当风点烛之象。

凡事虚名不利也。

签诗

对着风点蜡烛当然点不燃，即使点燃了，烛光疏影，如同镜中的恍惚之花。儿童看了几度要来收拾，但这一切只是收获虚名幻影而已。

签解

此签为中签。

富贵由天注定，贫穷为命中带来，不需要尽心费力地去追求谋划，

一切都是前世已经确定了的。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 97Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Fleeting shadows cast by a candle in the wind,

Reflect in the mirror like a bed of many flowers.

Mystified, the child wants to keep the flowers,

Only to find that these are only virtual and unreal.

Interpret­ation

It is predestined how wealth is distributed and Who will be poor.

Since it’s already decided, it is pointless to seek anything.

Meaning Of This Lot

As a lighted candle flickers in the wind, it is not good to aim for an empty title.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

It is not possible to light a candle in the wind. Even if one manages to do so, the candle flame will only cast fleeting shadows, like vague reflections of flowers in a mirror. Children would look at it several times and want to pick it up, but this is all an illusion.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Wealth is predestined by Heaven. Poverty is brought about by one's fate. There is no need to waste your effort in pursuing and scheming. Everything has been determined by your past lives.
