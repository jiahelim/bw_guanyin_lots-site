第 69 签吉凶：中签

签诗
冬来岭上一枝梅叶落枝枯终不摧但得阳春悄急至依然还我作花魁
解曰
一箭射空当空不空待等春来彩在其中
诗意

此卦梅花占魁之象，

凡事宜迟则吉也。

签诗

即使成了寒冬的梅树，虽然叶落枝空也终究不会被摧毁。只要春的气息悄悄到来，依旧会成为花中魁首。

签解

此签为中签。

一箭射向空中，其实并不是漫无目标。

梅花待到春天到来时，也会在五彩缤纷中放出光彩。

  • 自身情况应积善祈福
  • 投资理财谨慎小心
  • 经商买卖等待时机
  • 工农商业兴旺
  • 工作事业稳扎稳打
  • 学业考试应把握学习时光
  • 爱情婚姻晚些会成功
  • 怀孕生育春季平安
  • 出门在外到达目的地的时间会延迟
  • 畜养动物兴旺
  • 寻人找到
  • 寻失物在东方
  • 官司诉讼会亏输
  • 转换变更此时还不急，耐心等待。
  • 治病健康有虚惊
  • 祖坟牌位宜修缮
签语

此卦梅花占魁之象。所祈求之事应推迟进行，才会吉利。

Lot Verse

A plum tree on a hill on a winter’s day.

Its leaves have fallen, its twigs have withered, yet it stands.

When the warmth of Spring quietly returns,

Its floral majesty would once again be restored.

Interpret­ation

An arrow is released but misses its target.

All is not lost, when Spring is here again, your reward will be amongst what it brings.

Meaning Of This Lot

The plum tree blooms in full majesty.

Things may take a while to come to fruition but it will bode well.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Even if it becomes a plum tree in the cold winter, its leaves have fallen and its branches are bare, yet it remains standing. When the warmth of spring creeps in, the plum tree’s floral majesty will again be restored.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

An arrow is shot in the air but is not without a target. With the arrival of spring, the plum tree will radiate with splendour amid the profusion of spring colours.
