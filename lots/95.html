第 95 签吉凶：中签

签诗
志气功业在朝朝今将酒色不胜饶若见金鸡报君语钱财福禄与君招
解曰
志气心动守己未迟金鸡报语无不欢喜
诗意

此卦志气功名之象。

凡事守常大吉也。

签诗

有志向和才气的人，追求功业，应当天天努力，从现在起就不要贪酒恋色。如果某天有喜讯传来，钱财福禄可以随你挑选。

签解

此签为中签。

一方面要心怀高远的志向，另一方面也要脚踏实地，安守自己的本分。

等到金鸡报语，喜讯来临，便没有不令人欢悦的事情了。

Lot Verse

Rome was not built in a day.

Let no woman nor wine bury your way.

Rises to work when the golden rooster crows,

Money and fortune will soon be called.

Interpret­ation

Determine your goals, keep at it.

Diligence will be rewarded.

The golden rooster calls and happiness will come.

Meaning Of This Lot

Work hard to fulfill your ambitions.

Maintain things as-is and everything will turn out very well.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Those with ambitions and talents, in pursuit of merits & accomplishments, should work diligently daily. From now on, they should stay away from alcohol and women. When good news comes, you can pick and choose your wealth and happiness.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

While you can have high aspirations, you also need to keep your feet firmly planted on the ground ie. be realistic, and do your duty. When the golden rooster brings good news, everything will be joyous and blissful.
