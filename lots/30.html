第 30 签吉凶：中签

签诗
劝君切莫向他求似鹤飞来暗箭投若去采薪蛇在草恐遭毒口也忧愁
解曰
闭口合齿他事莫理若有亏心到头害己
诗意

此卦安份守己之象。

凡事小心谨防也。

签诗

不要不择对象就随便求人，以防别人表面相助却投来暗箭。正如拾柴，要提防被草中的毒蛇咬伤，带来忧患。

签解

此签为中签。

闭上嘴巴，合上牙齿，对不关乎自己的事情不要去理会。

如果做了亏心事，到头来也定然会祸害到自己。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 30Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Thou shalt not seek help from just anybody,

Beware of a hidden arrow disguised as a flying crane.

When gathering firewood in the field, a snake may lie in wait.

With venom that brings none but grief.

Interpret­ation

Keep your mouth shut. Don’t get involved in others’ affairs. You will pay the price if you do anything against your conscience.

Meaning Of This Lot

Be content with your lot & not be ambitious.

Be careful and cautious in everything.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Do not indiscriminately seek help from just anyone. This is to avoid falling into the trap of people pretending to help you on the surface, yet casting a secret arrow from behind. Just like when gathering firewood, one must guard against being bitten by poisonous snakes in the grass, which will bring misery.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Keep your mouth shut and do not get involved in affairs that do not concern you. You will surely pay the price if you do anything against your conscience.
