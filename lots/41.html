第 41 签吉凶：中签

签诗
无限好言君须记恰如认贼作为子莫贪眼下有些甜更虑他时还受苦
解曰
只这一着君子莫错牢记在心不生寂寞
诗意

此卦认贼作子之象。

凡事认真防假。

签诗

特别好的话你该记住，更不要干出认贼作子的事情。不要贪图眼前的一点儿甜头，要考虑将来怎么收场。

签解

此签为中签。

认贼作子这些事，凡是有眼光的人都不会这么做，要时时牢记在心，这样才不会在某个时候心生担忧。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 41Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Endless meaningful words you do take in,

Yet you mistaken the thief for a son.

Do not hunger after the spoils you see now,

Look into the past for lessons you must remember.

Interpret­ation

A single mistake can translate into a lasting regret.

Remember this and you will not be left out.

Meaning Of This Lot

Like a man who is tricked into treating a thief like his own son. Be careful not to be deceived by falsehood.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

You must remember especially good advice given. More importantly, do not treat a thief as your son. Do not be deceived by a little goodness you see in the present. It is necessary to consider carefully about the future consequences.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Any wise man with foresight would not make the mistake of treating a thief as a son. This you should always remember. Then you won’t have to worry all the time.
