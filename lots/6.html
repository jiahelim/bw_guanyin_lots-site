第 6 签吉凶：中签

签诗
投身岩下雕鸟居须是还他大丈夫拾得营谋谁可得通行天地此人无
解曰
食用无余身清喜贫待时利乐权与概人
诗意

此卦投岩铜鸟之象。

凡事宜顺吉之兆。

签诗

得此签者，如栖身居下的大雕一样，虽身处险境，却不失大丈夫的心胸与气概，所以你所获得的富贵荣禄，别人难以得到。最终你通行天地的能力也无人能比。

签解

此签为中签。

吃的用的少有富余，却自身清正，安贫乐道。

待到时机来临，获得的好处与快乐，也应衡量着帮助他人。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 6Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Sacrifice yourself to dwell in a Condor’s cave,

Perilous it may be but the stout heart that you are,

Reap rewards that none can match,

And none will roam the world as free as you.

Interpret­ation

Food will be ample though there is little excess. Life is poor and simple but contented. Be patient and enjoy what you have, share your wealth and power with others.

Meaning Of This Lot

Residing in a cave with a large condor. Live life in accordance with your lot and fortune will follow.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Receiver of this lot is like a condor seeking shelter. Despite being in a perilous place, you did not lose the stout heart and spirit of a true man. Therefore, the riches, glory and fortune that you achieve is something no one else can do. It means your ability to traverse heaven and earth is also unmatchable by others.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Your resources may be scarce, but you lead a contented and upright life.

When opportunity strikes you gain benefits and happiness, which you should also share with and help others.
