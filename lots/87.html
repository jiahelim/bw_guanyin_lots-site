第 87 签吉凶：中签

签诗
人行半岭日衔山峻岭崖岩未可安仰望上天为护佑此身犹在太平间
解曰
凿石见玉淘沙见金须要着力只是劳心

另：困厄之境。无处安身。求神庇佑。祈可放心。

诗意

此卦淘沙见金之象。

凡事有贵人之兆也。

签诗

才行至半山腰，太阳便要下山了，在悬崖陡岩间行走，不要期望会很平安。只有仰头祈求上天保佑，才可能平平安安。

签解

此签为中签。

如果想要凿开石头见到宝玉，淘尽黄沙见到黄金，则必须下大功夫，

当然这更是费心劳力的事情了。

Lot Verse

One is barely half-way up the mountains when the sun begins to set.

Dangerously stranded among perilously steep cliffs,

Skyward, one looks to heaven for protection,

And refuge on earth will soon be given.

Interpret­ation

Jade is revealed only after the stone is chiseled.

Gold is found after the sand is sifted.

You need to put in some effort.

(Alternative: Trapped in a bad situation with no safe place to hide. Seek protection from the Gods and you would be fine.)

Meaning Of This Lot

Sifts the sand to find gold for one caught in a dangerous situation.

A good omen where you can expect help from someone of influence.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

The mountaineer only reaches the middle ridge when the sun begins to set, so do not expect to be safe when walking along the steep perilous cliffs. The only way to be safe and sound is to pray for blessings to Heaven and ask the gods for help.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

If you want to chisel the stone to see the precious jade, pan the sand to see the gold, you need to make great efforts.
