第 57 签吉凶：中签

签诗
说是说非风过耳好衣好禄自然丰君莫记取当年事汝意还如我意同
解曰
所用谋望皆大欢喜从公用力如子逢娘
诗意

此卦孩儿见母之象。

诸事贵人大吉也。

签诗

听到是非之言就当做耳边风，这样自然会有富裕丰足的生活。莫再记着当年的旧事了。

签解

此签为中签。

对未来所作的谋划能够获得完满的结果。

做事恳切，以公为先，就像孩子遇到母亲般，将会获得依靠与协助。

  • 学业考试勤奋有成
  • 爱情婚姻双方相合
  • 怀孕生育女孩
  • 出门在外在途中受阻
  • 畜养动物有损失
  • 寻人难找到
  • 寻失物无法找到
  • 官司诉讼适宜和解
  • 迁徙搬家原地不动
  • 转换变更保持现况为佳
  • 治病健康宜积善祈福庇佑
  • 祖坟牌位吉利
签语

此卦孩儿见母之象。所祈求之事逢贵人相助则会大吉大利。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 57Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Let rumors or scandals whisk past your ears like winds,

For a life of fine clothes and plenty is yours to have.

Unshackle your past and let bygones be bygones,

On this, I am certain you will surely agree.

Interpret­ation

What you are planning will bring much success and joy to yourself and others.

Work for the good of the public with a devotion like a mother to her child.

Meaning Of This Lot

Like when the child sees the mother, you will have support from others, your pursuits will go well.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Turn a deaf ear to gossip and rumours, you will naturally gain happiness and abundance in your life. Avoid dwelling on the past.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

You will be able to realize your future plans. Do things sincerely, put the public first, serve them like the devotion of a mother to her child and you will find refuge and support.
