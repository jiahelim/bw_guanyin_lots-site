第 85 签吉凶：中签

签诗
云深雾罩山前路春残花尽又再开若得诗书沉梦醒贵人指引步天台
解曰
老者用求春残再开欲求名利晚遇横财
诗意

此封春尽花开之象。

凡事主后改变也。

签诗

云雾笼罩，前途迷茫，春尽花谢又再开。如果现在的酣梦能被诗书唤醒，再遇贵人指引便必定能步入仙境。（“天台”指天宫或天台山。）

签解

此签为中签。

若是老年人求得此签，则会获得第二春。

想要求问功名利禄，则要晚年才可获得意外的财富。

Lot Verse

Dark clouds and fogs shroud the mountain path.

Flowers wither and bloom again when spring is nearing its end.

As families unite, the moon is full again.

If you wake now from a deep poetic dream,

You will be led to the steps of Heaven.

Interpret­ation

The aged will bloom again as what remains after Spring does.

If you seek fame and fortune, you may claim a large fortune in later years.

Meaning Of This Lot

End of Spring as flowers bloom.

Things will prove promising in the end after a change.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Dark clouds and mist shroud the road ahead, the future is indistinct, flowers wither and bloom again when spring is nearing its end. If you can wake up from your current poetic dream, and once again receive a nobleman’s guidance, then you will surely enter into a Heavenly paradise.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

If an elderly person receives this lot, he or she will have a second chance at romance. If you seek fame and wealth, then you may get unexpected fortune in your later years.
