第 10 签吉凶：中签

签诗
石藏无价玉和珍只管他乡外客寻宛如持灯更觅火不如收拾枉劳心
解曰
姻缘会遇何事不成须无限意眼前是真
诗意

此卦持灯觅火之象。

凡事待时成就也。

签诗

自己就是石头中蕴藏的珍贵宝玉，颇富才智，却一味的奢望于贵人相助和异地求财，这多么像拿着灯找火，十足的枉劳心思。

签解

此签为中签。

所有的姻缘都会出现，做任何事情均可成功。

虽可以不限制自己的想象力，但得相信，自己眼前的一切才是真切的。

Lot Verse

Hidden within are priceless jade and gems,

Yet one searches in foreign lands for the same.

Like one who seeks light while holding a lamp,

Save your troubles, pack up and head home.

Interpret­ation

Marriage will come, everything can be accomplished.

Don’t limit yourself, what is before you is real and genuine.

Meaning Of This Lot

A man holds a lamp and still looks for light. Be patient and wait, success will be at hand.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

You are the precious jade hidden in the stone. While you possess talents and wisdom, yet you constantly desire for benefactor help, and seek wealth in other lands. This is just like holding a lamp to seek light, a complete waste of effort.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

All that is fated will come, doing anything can be successful. Although you do not need to limit your imagination, you have to believe what is before you is real and genuine.
