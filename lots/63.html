第 63 签吉凶：中签

签诗
昔然行船失了针今朝依旧海中寻若然寻得原针在也费工夫也费心
解曰
福是禄基禄是福种说得分明切宜守谨
诗意

此卦海中寻针之象。

凡事费心劳力也。

签诗

从前在船上丢失了针，现在到海里去寻找。即使找到从前那根针，也是枉费心力和时间。

签解

此签为中签。

福分是财禄的基础，财禄是福分的种子，二者相辅相成。

既然已经清楚分明，那么就应该谨慎处理。

Lot Verse

A needle lost when we were last at sea,

Now searching for it, we continue at sea.

A waste of efforts and labor,

Even if we do find the needle.

Interpret­ation

Fortune is based on wealth as wealth is built on fortune.

Know this and proceed with caution.

Meaning Of This Lot

Searching for a needle in the vast ocean.

A futile and laborious effort.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

You lost a needle on a ship before and now you are still searching for it in the sea. Even if the old needle is found, it is a waste of effort and time.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Blessing is the foundation for wealth and wealth is the seed for blessing. They complement each other. Since these two are already clear and distinct, they should be handled with caution.
