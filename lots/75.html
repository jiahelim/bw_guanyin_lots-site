签诗
恰如抱虎过高山战战兢兢胆碎寒幸得山前逢妙手方能保得一身安
解曰
危危险险前程有阻若问求谋到底辛苦
诗意

此卦抱虎过山之象。

凡事险凶惊恐也。

签诗

像抱着老虎爬高山，真是战战兢兢、胆破心寒。幸好在前方遇得高人相助，万事无虞，人也因此一生平安。

签解

此签为中签。

危险就在眼前，胆战心惊，前程受阻。

如果要询问如何计谋策划，终究是很辛苦的事。

Interpret­ation

There are dangers and obstacles in your path.

Meaning Of This Lot

Like a man carrying a tiger up the mountains,

there is fear and danger ahead.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

There is a feeling of fear and trepidation, like carrying a tiger to climb a mountain, frightening one to death. Fortunately, you meet an expert benefactor up front, so everything is safe and you can lead a safe life.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Danger is ahead, tremble with fear, the future is perilous and full of obstacles. If your query is regarding planning and strategy, the answer is: it will be a very difficult process.
