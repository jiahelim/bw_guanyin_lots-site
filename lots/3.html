第 3 签吉凶：下签

签诗
临风冒雨去还乡正是其身似燕儿衔得泥来欲作垒到头垒坏复须泥
解曰
千般用计晨昏不停谁知此事到底劳心
诗意

此卦燕子衔泥之象。

凡事劳心费力也。

签诗

经历无数挫折，不辞辛苦，忙忙碌碌地奔波劳作，就像燕子顶风冒雨衔泥筑巢，但筑成的巢不断被毁坏，不得不反复衔泥重新筑巢。

签解

此签为下签。

虽然心机用尽，从早到晚忙个不停，但结果仍不会有更多收获，只是白白地劳心费力罢了。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 3Prophesy: Inauspicious

Lot Verse

A traveller returns, having braved the winds and rains.

Laboring like a homing swallow,

Which builds in vain a shelter of clay,

That collapses and disappears whenever it rains.

Interpret­ation

Even when one tries every way and means, slogs hard day and night, all efforts will eventually be in vain.

Meaning Of This Lot

Like a swallow which tries to build a nest with clay to protect itself against the rains, everything is laborious

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

One goes through countless setbacks, undeterred by hardships, rushing and busy working, just like a swallow who braves the wind and rain to build its nest with clay. However, the nest is constantly being damaged and the swallow has no choice but to repeatedly rebuild the nest.

Divination of The Lot

This is an inauspicious lot.

Although you try every means working day and night, in the end, there are no additional gains and all efforts will turn out to be fruitless.
