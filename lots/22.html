签诗
旱时田里皆枯槁谢天甘雨落淋淋花果草木皆润泽始知一雨值千金
解曰
田蚕倍熟命运渐来病遇良药行人便回
诗意

此卦旱逢甘雨之象。

凡事难中有救也。

签诗

干旱致使田间禾苗都干枯了，一场好雨降临，花果树木都得到滋润，才明白这一场雨价值千金，非常可贵。

签解

此签为中签。

农作物的收成翻倍，好运也渐渐来临。

如果得病也必能得遇良药，外出远行的人也即将返回。

Lot Verse

The paddies were dried during the drought.

Thanks to the heavens, a heavy shower finally came.

The flowers, fruits and plants are now nourished,

Worth its weight in gold, this shower promised.

Interpret­ation

A bountiful crop harvest. Luck is gradually improving.

The sick will find a beneficial cure.

The traveler may return.

Meaning Of This Lot

This lot uses the image of drought meeting sweet rain to imply there is salvation in all difficulties.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

The drought has caused the grain crop seedlings to wither. After a bout of rain, the trees, flowers and fruits are nourished. From this we understood that this bout of rain is worth a thousand gold and is very precious.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Harvesting of farmed crops will be doubled. Good luck is also around the corner. Should you be sick, you will certainly get good medicine Those who travel afar will also return soon.
