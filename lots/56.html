第 56 签吉凶：中签

签诗
滩小石溪流水响清风明月贵人忙路须借问何方去莫取林中花草香
解曰
营谋用事劳心费力守旧待时凡事大吉
诗意

此封船行小滩之象。

凡事有贵人助也。

签诗

乘一叶小舟悠游在小溪浅滩间，山林中风清月明，花草飘香。若问前路何去何从，不如享受着山林乐趣，等贵人出现。

签解

此签为中签。

谋划改变当前的现状，并去实施，是费力劳心的事情。

保持现状，等待时机再作打算，才可能大吉大利。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 56Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Running water sounds on stones at the shallows of streams,

Disturb not the wise on a cool, clear moonlit night.

Need seek guidance on which path to go,

But not be distracted by the scent and sight of flowers.

Interpret­ation

Efforts spent in organizing your plans to achieve a goal will be wasted. You should let things remain for now and wait for a time when things would augur well.

Meaning Of This Lot

Riding a small boat along a stream, you will have benefactors to help you through.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Row a boat leisurely in between streams and shoals, enjoy the clear breeze, the bright moon and the fragrance of flowers in the mountain. If you wish to ask which path to go in life, it is better to relax, enjoy the beauty of the mountain and its flora, and wait for guidance from a benefactor.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

It is extremely tiring, both mentally and physically to plan and implement changes to the present situation. It is better to keep the status quo, wait for the right time to plan then everything can be auspicious.
