第 46 签吉凶：中签

签诗
劝君耐守旧生涯把定心肠勿起歹直待有人轻着力枯枝老树再生花
解曰
守旧守静事自成就动则多殃静则无咎
诗意

此卦枯木生花之象。

凡事自有成就也。

签诗

耐心持守原有的良好品质，不要产生过多的妄想。等待有人提携佐助，就会像枯树开花一样时来运转，也自然会兴旺发达。

签解

此签为中签。

坚持原有的行事原则，事情自然能取得成功。

如果变来变去，则可能招致祸殃，反而是安静自处，没有灾难。

  • 转换变更宜保持原状

  • 祖坟牌位吉利

此卦枯木生花之象。所祈求之事能顺应自然便会有好结果。

Lot: 46Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Stay still and maintain your life as-is,

Steel your heart but create no evil.

Wait until help becomes available,

Flowers will bloom again in a withered tree.

Interpret­ation

Keep things as they are. Do not change anything and nothing will go wrong. Things will eventually fall in place. Any movement now may bring much misfortune.

Meaning Of This Lot

A tree that had withered blossomed again.

Things will naturally fall in place.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

Be patient and keep your current good qualities, do not have too much wishful thinking. When someone brings you up, it is like a withered tree blossoming again, progress and prosperity will come naturally.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Maintain the original ethical principles and things will naturally be well. If one keeps changing, it may bring misfortune. On the contrary, stay quiet and maintain things as status quo then there will be no misfortune.
