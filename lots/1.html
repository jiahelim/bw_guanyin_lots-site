Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 1Prophesy: Auspicious

Lot Verse

The world begins and calls for opportunity, and things will fall into place at the right time,

obtaining this lot is extremely auspicious, just like the King rewards the loyal and righteous ones.

Interpret­ation

You are ready, the conditions are favorable, seize the opportunities, prosperity and success will be yours as the Goddess Guan Yin has indicated.

Meaning Of This Lot

In the Pan Gu legend the creation of the world is described where all creatures will be given life.

It signifies abundance and fortune in all matters.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

You will be experiencing the best opportunity in life soon. However, in order to succeed, hard work is still required. Everything can be accomplished at the right time. Therefore, you need to be patient. Believe that achievements will take place with efforts made. It is not easy to receive this lot, and as long as one is upright, loyal and has integrity, you will be appreciated by your superior. Thus, you should make unremitting efforts towards your goal.

Divination of The Lot

This is an auspicious lot.

Good luck is coming soon. Although good luck will come at year end, Guan Yin Bodhisattva has already given good omen here and revealed this auspicious news to you in advance.
