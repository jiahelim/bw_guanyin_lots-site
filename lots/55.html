第 55 签吉凶：中签

签诗
父贤传子子传孙衣食丰隆只靠天堂上椿萱人快乐饥饭渴饮困时眠
解曰
接竹引泉流传不绝君子谋望无不欣悦
诗意

此卦接竹引泉之象。

凡事谋望大吉也。

签诗

贤能代代相传，都过着丰衣足食的生活，这都是天的护佑。父母安康全家欢乐，饿了吃，渴了喝，困倦就睡。

签解

此签为中签。

就像用竹节引泉水一样，一节接一节，连续不断。贤能的人会筹谋未来。

因此，无不有令人欣喜欢悦的结果。

Goddess Of Mercy Divine Lots

Lot: 55Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

A virtuous father will teach his descendants virtues.

Heaven will provide plenty for their wants and needs.

The elders in the family are happy and fulfilled.

Hunger is filled, thirst is quenched, the tired may rest,

Interpret­ation

Like bamboo poles arranged to connect to a spring, this will survive generations. Whatever an upright man pursues, it will be filled with great joy and happiness.

Meaning Of This Lot

Stack the bamboo to lead towards a stream.

What you aspire will be fortuitously achieved.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

It is the blessing from Heaven that a virtuous father can pass on his virtues to descendants with generations all living a life of abundance. Parents are healthy, the family is happy, with sufficient food to eat, drink and can rest when tired.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

It is like drawing water from spring by joining sections of bamboo together. The able man knows to plan for his future. Hence the ending is always filled with joy and happiness.
