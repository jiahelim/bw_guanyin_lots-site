第 20 签吉凶：中签

签诗
当春久雨喜开晴玉兔金乌渐渐明旧事消散新事遂看看一跳过龙门
解曰
神佛护持有灾无危途生平安到底荣归
诗意

此卦久雨初明之象。

凡事遂意也。

签诗

正当春天，绵绵细雨终于停了，天空突然放晴，太阳与月亮开始变得明朗。过去的事情过去了，新开始的事都很顺，万象更新，如同鱼跳过了龙门。

签解

此签为中签。

有神灵、菩萨的保佑护持，即使有灾难发生，也没有实质性的危险。

一生平安，最后能获得荣华富贵。

Lot Verse

As the Spring weather clears after a wet spell,

The Golden Crow and Jade Rabbit can now be seen.

As the old dissipates, the new arrives,

One jump is all it needs to clear the Dragon Gate.

Interpret­ation

Protected by celestial beings, disasters may come but there will be no danger.

Your whole life, you will be safe and sound, and enjoy honors and glory at the end.

Meaning Of This Lot

The image of clear weather after continuous rain, signifies that everything will be in your favor.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

With the arrival of spring, the continuous drizzle finally stops. The sky suddenly lightens. The sun and the moon begin to become brighter. What is past has passed. The future as a new beginning is smooth sailing. All things take on a new aspect, like a fish that leaps over the dragon gate.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Deities and bodhisattvas give their protection and support. Thus, even if disasters happen, there is no apparent danger. One will be safe and sound throughout one’s life. Ultimately, one will acquire status and wealth.
