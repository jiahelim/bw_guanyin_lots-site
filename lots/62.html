第 62 签吉凶：中签

签诗
晨昏全赖佛扶持须是逢危却不危若得贵人相引处那时财帛亦相随
解曰
平平稳稳四方名显改旧从新亦无凶险
诗意

此卦神佛暗佑之象。

凡事忍耐大吉也。

签诗

时时刻刻得到佛祖庇佑，即使遇到危险也不会产生严重后果。如果再得到贵人提携，财富必定会随之而来。

签解

此签为中签。

平平稳稳，而且名扬四方。

即使改变原有状况，开始新的人生，也不会有什么风险。

Lot Verse

Chants the words of Buddha mornings and evenings,

With his support we may meet dangers but will avert all.

Guidance and support from a distinguished patron,

Will bring you wealth and gold as well.

Interpret­ation

A steady and stable situation.

Your name is known in all four directions.

There is little danger even if you do change the old for the new.

Meaning Of This Lot

Unseen divine protection.

You need not worry about anything but wait and good fortune will come.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

With the protection of the Buddha at all times, you would not be severely hurt, even if you encounter danger. Additionally, if you have the help of a patron, wealth and abundance will definitely follow.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Steady and well-known, even if you change the original situation and start a new life, there will be no risk.
