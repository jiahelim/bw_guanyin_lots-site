第 34 签吉凶：中签

签诗
行藏出入礼义恭言必忠良信必从心不了然且静澈光明红日正当空
解曰
心中无事秋水澄清不须疑惑事自然成
诗意

此卦红日当空正照之象。

凡事遂意也。

签诗

任何时候都讲礼义，为人谦恭，语言真诚、善良的人，确实让人信从。内心不明白时，姑且静心省虑，事情自然会清楚，事业也自然会兴旺。

签解

此签为中签。

心中不要想得太多，应该像秋水一样澄清明静，更不必疑神疑鬼，事情自然会水到渠成。

Lot: 34Prophesy: Neutral

Lot Verse

Conduct your life with dignity and propriety,

Speak and act with honesty and sincerity.

A heart that harbors no malice has clarity,

As bright as the rays of the Sun will be.

Interpret­ation

Do not burden the heart needlessly. It is clear and tranquil like waters in autumn. Do not doubt anymore as the natural course of things is such that things will work out eventually.

Meaning Of This Lot

The Sun shines brightly in the middle of the day.

Everything will be as you wish.

Lot Verse Interpret­ation

It is advisable to observe etiquette at all times, be humble, sincere and kind then people will indeed trust you. Even if you have doubts, you can discern calmly, things will naturally be clear, then your business will naturally prosper.

Divination of The Lot

This is a neutral lot.

Do not worry too much, your mind should be clear and tranquil like the water in autumn. Do not even doubt and be suspicious as the natural course of things is that it will work out eventually.
